## How it was made

Rendered frame by frame from code in Chrome (30 fps, 1920×1080), timed to the voiceover's word timings. The voice is a synthetic neural voice reading the script. The music bed and sound effects are synthesised in code, so there is nothing licensed in it. English captions are provided as a separate file.
