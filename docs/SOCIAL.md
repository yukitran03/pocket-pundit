## LinkedIn

I shipped Pocket Pundit for the Tatum × Walrus Total Recall hackathon.

It's a Telegram bot for football fans who can't watch everything. Every evening it sends the three matches worth your time, inside the hours you can actually watch, with no spoilers and a ping when a game turns. One tap after full time teaches it your taste.

What I'm proudest of is that the memory is real and checkable. Each rating is an encrypted Walrus Memory entry on Sui mainnet. Every brief is a blind A/B test, locked on Walrus before kick-off. The proof page reads everything live through Tatum's Sui gateway.
